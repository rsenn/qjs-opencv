Tanqram into readable QR-code
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This is a general description and definition of:

Method of detection of Tanqram which is a valid QR-code

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
       

     |Graphic Analysys on Bitmap or Video stream
     |
     | Requirements
       [
        A bitmap or video frame with Black/White Tanqram-QR
        Each tangram shape has black outline

        7-Shapes: 5 triangles, a square and a parallelogram
       ]
              

- Apply a canny edge-detection algorithm on the image
       - Perform a cv::findContours 
     ]
